0:20-0:21. The workflow drafts; people merge. It can read and propose, and that's it. Its writes go through a short allow-list: draft pull requests into the docs repo, and a fallback issue if that fails. The agent's reasoning is fuzzy; what it's allowed to do is not. Merging stays with people.

How well does it work? I wrote this up on the GitHub blog with Peli de Halleux. From May 3 to June 2 the workflow ran on 396 merged product PRs and opened 82 docs PRs. All 82 merged, median 44.8 hours. The PR we just walked through took about two days, close to that median. Those are reported numbers, not a promise.
